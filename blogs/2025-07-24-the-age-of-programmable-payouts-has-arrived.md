With the passing of the GENIUS Act in Congress, the future of programmable money is no longer theoretical. For the first time, there is a clear regulatory framework in the United States that recognises the role of stablecoins in modern finance. For those of us who have been building infrastructure around digital identity, wallets, and stablecoin disbursements for years, this is a defining moment.
