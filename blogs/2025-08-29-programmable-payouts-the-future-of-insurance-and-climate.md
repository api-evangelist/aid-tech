For decades, payouts in insurance and government programmes have followed the same path: slow, manual, and often opaque. A storm destroys a home, a flood wipes out a harvest, a wildfire forces families to relocate, and then the waiting begins. Weeks, sometimes months, pass before claims are processed and payments reach the people who need them most.
